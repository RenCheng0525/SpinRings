# Spin Rings 轉盤方塊

Live Demo: [https://rencheng0525.github.io/SpinRings/](https://rencheng0525.github.io/SpinRings/)

Generated by Claude with prompts only, no manual code editing.

> 💿 A falling-block puzzle played on a spinning vinyl record. Blocks drop from the top and fall toward the center. You don't move the block, you spin the record underneath it. Inspired by classic falling-block puzzles, dressed up as a 1970s hi-fi turntable.

## Overview

A web-based **circular falling-block puzzle** in a single HTML file. The playfield is a ring of 16 columns and 12 layers wrapped around the label of a vinyl record. The block always stays at 12 o'clock, so spinning the disc is how you line it up. Everything is drawn with **Canvas 2D**, and the music and sound effects are synthesized in real time. There are no image or audio files.

## Features

### 💿 A Board That Spins

- **Ring Playfield**: 16 columns around, 12 layers deep. Blocks fall inward, from the outer edge toward the center label.
- **Always on Top**: The falling block never leaves 12 o'clock. Left and right spin the whole record, so you always know where the block will land.
- **Wraparound**: There is no left or right wall. A block can sit across the seam between the last column and the first.
- **Clear a Full Ring**: Fill all 16 cells of a layer to clear it. The outer layers then slide one step toward the center.
- **Red Dotted Line**: The outer edge of the record is the limit. If a block locks outside it, the game is over.
- **Ghost Preview**: A dashed outline shows exactly where the current block will land.
- **Next Block**: The next block is always shown on the display.
- **Fair Shuffle**: The seven block shapes are dealt from a shuffled bag, so you never wait too long for the one you need.

### 🎛️ A 1970s Turntable

- **Brushed Aluminum Deck**: The whole game is one piece of hardware sitting on a dark shelf.
- **Vinyl Record**: Fine grooves, plus a light sheen that stays still while the record turns, just like real vinyl under a lamp.
- **Platter and Strobe Dots**: The silver platter rim carries strobe dots that turn with the record.
- **Center Label**: A cream label with a red mark, so you can see how far the record has turned.
- **Fluorescent Display (VFD)**: Score, lines, level and the next block glow green on dark glass. Unlit segments show faintly as "8", like real vacuum fluorescent tubes.
- **Physical Keys**: Buttons sink when pressed. Pressing a keyboard key also presses the matching button on screen.

### 🎵 Music and Sound

- **Synthesized Soundtrack**: A lo-fi groove looping Am → F → C → G, with kick, snare, hi-hat, bass, arpeggio, chord pad and a little vinyl crackle.
- **Speeds Up With You**: The tempo starts at 92 BPM and rises by 6 BPM every level, up to 150.
- **Eight Sound Effects**: Spin, rotate, lock, drop, ring clear (more notes for more rings), level up, start and game over.
- **One Sound Button**: Cycles through *Music + SFX*, *SFX only* and *Muted*. A compressor keeps loud moments from distorting.

### 🤖 AI Demo

- **Thinks Like a Player**: Tries all 4 rotations in all 16 columns and scores each result by stack height, covered holes, bumpiness, rings cleared and distance from the red line. Bumpiness wraps around, because the board is a circle.
- **Plays at a Smart Kid's Pace**: Thinks for about half a second, then presses one key at a time (about 4 to 5 presses a second), pauses to double-check, then drops. It plays perfectly at low levels and starts running out of time as the speed climbs.
- **Take Over Anytime**: Press any control, or touch the record, and the AI hands the game back to you.
- **Endless Demo**: After a game over, the AI starts a new game by itself 2.5 seconds later. It makes a nice attract screen.
- **Fair High Scores**: A game the AI touched never counts toward your high score.

### 📊 Scoring System

| Points | Event |
|--------|-------|
| 100 × level | Clear 1 ring |
| 300 × level | Clear 2 rings at once |
| 500 × level | Clear 3 rings at once |
| 800 × level | Clear 4 rings at once |
| Level up | Every 5 rings cleared |

Blocks fall one layer every 1000 × 0.86^(level − 1) milliseconds, never faster than 110 ms.

### 🎯 Controls

**Keyboard**

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| ← → | Spin the record |
| ↑ / X | Rotate the block clockwise |
| Z | Rotate the block counter-clockwise |
| ↓ | Move down one layer |
| Space | Drop instantly (also starts a game) |
| P / Esc | Pause |
| M | Sound: Music + SFX / SFX only / Muted |
| A | Toggle the AI demo |
| ? | Open the help window |

**Touch** (the layout changes automatically on touch screens)

| Input | Action |
|-----|--------|
| Swipe sideways on the record | Spin the record. It follows your finger around the center, like turning a real record |
| Tap the record | Rotate the block (or start and resume when not playing) |
| Swipe down on the record | Drop instantly |
| Second-finger tap while spinning | Drop instantly, so one hand can spin and drop |
| Rotate button (left) / Drop button (right) | Two large buttons, one for each thumb |

On touch screens, the left, right and down buttons are hidden, because the record itself does that job.

### ✨ Small Touches

- **Bilingual**: Traditional Chinese and English, chosen from your browser language and switchable at any time.
- **Help Window**: The **?** button opens a short guide. It shows touch controls on phones and keyboard controls on computers, and it pauses the game while open.
- **Auto Pause**: The game pauses when you switch tabs or windows.
- **Reduced Motion**: If your system asks for less motion, the record snaps instead of easing, and clears do not flash.
- **Phone Friendly**: Respects notches and home bars, and sizes itself to fit the screen.
- **Saved Settings**: High score, language and sound mode are remembered in your browser.

## 🛠 Technical Highlights

- **Polar Grid**: Each cell is a ring sector drawn with two arcs. Column positions wrap around with a simple modulo.
- **Spin Instead of Move**: Moving the block one column and rotating the view one column happen together, which is what keeps the block at the top. The displayed angle eases toward the target for smooth spinning.
- **Web Audio Scheduler**: Music is scheduled with a lookahead (every 25 ms, notes are queued 0.12 s ahead), so the beat stays steady even when the page is busy.
- **Gesture Recognition**: A swipe is classified in its first 12 pixels as either "spin" or "drop". Spinning tracks the finger's angle around the center, and is ignored too close to the middle, where the angle jumps around.
- **Text by Attribute**: Interface text lives in one table. HTML elements mark what they need with `data-text`, `data-html` and `data-label`, so switching language is one function.
- **Readable Code**: The script is split into numbered sections, from settings and text to game logic, drawing and controls, with comments in Traditional Chinese.
- **Single HTML File**: No build step and no libraries. Only the fonts (Noto Sans TC and VT323) come from Google Fonts, and the game falls back to system fonts without an internet connection.
